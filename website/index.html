Your phone counts the reps. You level up.

Levelex is a workout coach that watches through your camera. It counts push-ups, squats, burpees and ten more exercises, corrects your form out loud, and turns every rep into XP. Everything runs on your phone.

What it does

A coach in your pocket, not in the cloud

Camera rep counting

Thirteen exercises counted from the side, the front or a diagonal, in portrait or landscape. Fast moves like burpees and jumping jacks included.

A coach that talks

Live cues like “go lower” and “hips up”, a 3-2-1 countdown, every rep called out, and notes after the set on what to fix next time.

MY PT, your trainer

Pick a time and a focus. Get a warm-up, workout and cool-down built around the equipment you own, guided move by move with rest timers.

3D movement guides

A realistic 3D athlete demonstrates more than 60 movements and lights up the muscles that do the work. Drag to see any angle.

Levels and streaks

XP for every rep, a new daily challenge, streaks that respect your rest days, 30 badges and a weekly report. Climb from Rookie to Legend.

Strength log

Track loads, follow proven progression rules, catch personal records, and see which muscles have recovered. Import from Strong, Hevy or FitNotes.

Challenge friends

Take turns in front of one phone: most reps in a minute, or first to a target. The same counting for everyone, and a podium at the end.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no analytics. Camera frames are analysed on the phone and thrown away. Your history stays on your device.

How it works

Prop it up, move, level up

  1. 1

    Prop up your phone

    Two to three metres away, with your whole body in frame. Each exercise tells you where the phone works best.

  2. 2

    Move

    On-device pose detection follows 33 points on your body about 12 times a second. The coach counts, cues and keeps time.

  3. 3

    Level up

    Every rep earns XP. Finish the set, keep the streak, beat your best, and watch the level climb.

Why it counts the way you would

Each exercise is measured against your own top position. For push-ups that is how far your shoulders drop towards your hands; for squats, how far your hips drop towards your feet. That reads the same from the side, the front or a diagonal, near or far. Rules for what must move and what must stay planted stop waving arms, walking or a hand near the lens from ever counting, and a shallow rep is flagged instead of counted.

The counters are tested against recordings of Google's ML Kit pose detector from six phone placements. 71 of 78 recorded sets were counted exactly; the misses were floor exercises filmed from the head or feet, which is why the app suggests where to put the phone.

Privacy

What happens on your phone stays on your phone

Levelex has no servers to send anything to. There is no account to create, no analytics and no advertising.

  • Camera frames are analysed in memory and discarded. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.
  • Your face, if you register it, is stored as a list of numbers, never as a photo. Erase it any time.
  • Your history, photos and equipment stay in the app's private storage.
  • Backups and share cards leave the phone only when you share them yourself.
  • Permissions: the camera, notifications if you want reminders, and (optional, on Android) exact alarms for reminders on the minute. Nothing else.

Install

Get Levelex on your phone

Levelex is not in the app stores, so you install it from the project's official releases. Only download it from this site or github.com/khjabir/levelex/releases.

The first public build is on its way. Until it is published, you can build Levelex yourself (see “From source” below), or watch the GitHub repository to hear when it lands.

Levelex for Android

Android 7.0 or newer · latest release

Download APK

Older 32-bit phone? 32-bit APK · Not sure? Universal APK · Checksums

  1. Download the APK on your phone

    Open this page on the phone and tap Download APK. Nearly every phone from the last eight years is 64-bit, so that is the right file. If Android later says the app can't be installed, use the universal APK instead.

  2. Allow the install

    Open the downloaded file. Android asks for permission to install apps from your browser or Files app: tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, and go back. On Android 7, turn on Settings → Security → Unknown sources instead.

  3. Install

    Tap Install. Google Play Protect may warn that it doesn't recognise the app, because Levelex isn't on Google Play. Tap More details → Install anyway.

  4. Open Levelex

    Allow the camera when you start your first set, and notifications if you want reminders.

Updating

Download the newer APK and install it over the top. Your history stays. Don't uninstall first: uninstalling deletes the app's data (export a backup in Settings → Data & backup if you ever need to).

Checking the file

Each release lists SHA-256 checksums. On a computer, compare them with shasum -a 256 levelex-android-arm64.apk (macOS, Linux) or certutil -hashfile levelex-android-arm64.apk SHA256 (Windows).

Apple only lets App Store apps install directly, so on iPhone Levelex is signed with your own Apple ID, the same way developers test their apps. A free Apple ID works. You need iOS 15.5 or newer and a computer for the first install.

Option A · no coding, Windows or Mac

Sideload the app file

  1. Download levelex-ios-unsigned.ipa on your computer.

  2. Install Sideloadly or AltStore on the computer and connect the iPhone with a cable.

  3. Open the IPA in the tool and sign in with your Apple ID. It signs the app for your phone and installs it.

  4. On the iPhone, open Settings → General → VPN & Device Management and trust your Apple ID's developer app. On iOS 16 or newer, also turn on Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode and restart.

Option B · a Mac with Xcode

Build and run it yourself

  1. Install Xcode, CocoaPods and Flutter 3.47, then get the code:

    git clone https://github.com/khjabir/levelex.git
cd levelex
flutter pub get
cd ios && pod install && cd ..

  2. Open ios/Runner.xcworkspace in Xcode, choose your Apple ID as the Team under Signing & Capabilities, connect the iPhone and run:

    flutter run --release

  3. Trust the developer app and turn on Developer Mode as in Option A.

Keeping it working

Apps signed with a free Apple ID stop opening after 7 days until they are refreshed. AltStore can refresh over Wi-Fi while your computer is on; with Sideloadly or Xcode, install again. Your data stays either way. A paid Apple Developer account extends this to a year.

Levelex is a Flutter app. You need Flutter 3.47, plus JDK 17 and the Android SDK for Android, or a Mac with Xcode for iPhone.

git clone https://github.com/khjabir/levelex.git
cd levelex
flutter pub get
flutter run --release

To build an installable Android file instead:

flutter build apk --release --split-per-abi

Platform setup, signing and release builds are covered in docs/BUILDING.md.

FAQ

Questions

Why isn't Levelex on Google Play or the App Store?

It isn't in the stores yet. For now it is distributed directly through its GitHub releases, which is why this page has an install guide.

Is it safe to install an app from outside Google Play?

Only download Levelex from this site or the project's GitHub releases page, and compare the checksum if you want to be sure the file is untouched. The app asks for the camera, and for notifications if you want reminders. It doesn't send your data anywhere.

Does it cost anything?

No. There is no price, no subscription, no ads and no in-app purchases.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Pose detection, the face check, the trainer and the 3D guides all run on the phone.

Does it record video of me?

No. Each camera frame is analysed in memory to find your body's position and then discarded.

Why does it ask to register my face?

So that only you can score your own sets and streak. The face is stored as numbers, not a photo, and you can skip the check for any set (it is then saved as unverified and earns no XP).

Which phones work?

Android 7.0 or newer and iOS 15.5 or newer, with a working camera. Tracking is smoother on recent phones; phones with little memory get a lighter 3D guide automatically.

How accurate is the counting?

Very good with your whole body in frame and the phone where the exercise suggests. Floor exercises such as sit-ups and glute bridges count best filmed from the side or a diagonal, not from your head or feet. If a set ever miscounts, the summary screen can copy a tracking report (numbers only) to share on GitHub.

How do I move my history to a new phone?

In Settings → Data & backup, export a backup and open it with Levelex on the new phone. You can also import workout history from Strong, Hevy or FitNotes.
